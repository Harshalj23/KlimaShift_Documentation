## System Overview

- 3 Energy Meters × 5 channels = 15 channels (C1–C15)
- C1, C2, C3 reserved for 3-Phase Incoming Mains
- C4–C15 available for MCB mapping

## Requirements

- KlimaShift Operations App
- Access to Google Sheets for data recording
