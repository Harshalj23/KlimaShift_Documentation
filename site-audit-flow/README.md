## Audit Flow Summary

| Part | Steps | Description | Device |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|
| A | 1–8 | Client & Building Selection, Permissions | Phone |
| B | 9–12 | Building Details & Orientation | Phone |
| C | 13–16 | Store Front Photo | Phone |
| D | 17–20 | 3D Room Scan | **iPhone** |
| E | 21–34 | Asset Audit (Add & Verify Assets) | Phone |
| F | 35–45 | Electrical Panel Audit (MDB, MCB Config) | Phone + Multimeter |
| G | 46–54 | MCB Mapping (Switch MCBs, Map Assets) | Phone |
| H | 55–56 | End Audit | Phone |
| I | 57–61 | Post-Audit: Meter Installation Planning | Measuring Tape |

## Requirements

### Devices
- **iPhone** (12 or newer with LiDAR) — for 3D room scanning
- **Android Phone** — for KlimaShift Operations App
- **Multimeter** — for finding MCB phase

### Tools
- Tester
- Screwdriver Set
- Wire Strippers

### Safety Equipment (PPE)
- Safety Gloves (insulated rubber)
- Safety Shoes (electrically insulated)
- Safety Goggles
- Hard Hat
- Insulated Mat

## Safety Rules

- Never work on live panels without proper safety equipment
- Always inform site personnel before switching MCBs on/off
- Keep one hand in pocket when testing live circuits
- Disconnect power before opening panel covers if possible
