# Site Audit Flow — KlimaShift Operations App

Step-by-step guide for performing a complete site audit using the **KlimaShift Operations App**

> **iPhone Required for 3D Scan** | Android phones cannot perform 3D scans

---

# ⚠ Requirements — Before You Begin

> **Safety First!** Always wear proper safety equipment before performing any electrical work. Never touch live wires without proper PPE.

### Devices

- **iPhone** (12 or newer with LiDAR) — for 3D room scanning
- **Android Phone** — for KlimaShift Operations App (all other tasks)
- **Multimeter** — for finding MCB phase and checking voltage

### Tools

- **Tester** — for checking live/neutral connections
- **Screwdriver Set** — for opening panel covers and terminals
- **Wire Strippers** — if needed for any wiring work

### Safety Equipment (PPE)

- **Safety Gloves** — insulated rubber gloves for electrical work
- **Safety Shoes** — electrically insulated footwear
- **Safety Goggles** — eye protection from sparks or debris
- **Hard Hat** — head protection in industrial areas
- **Insulated Mat** — stand on while working on live panels

### Documentation & Others

- **Google Sheet Access** — for recording audit data
- **Site Access Permission** — client authorization letter if required
- **Notepad/Pen** — for quick notes during audit
- **Flashlight** — for dark areas and panel inspection

> **Electrical Safety Rules:**
> - Never work on live panels without proper safety equipment
> - Always inform site personnel before switching MCBs on/off
> - Keep one hand in pocket when testing live circuits (avoid current path through heart)
> - Disconnect power before opening panel covers if possible
> - If unsure about any electrical work, consult a qualified electrician

## Step 17: Start 3D Scan

Tap **"Start Scan"** to begin the 3D scanning process. Follow the instructions shown on the device — you will need to slowly pan the phone around the room.

![Start Scan](images/17.png)

### 3D Scan Instructions

1. Hold the iPhone at chest height, facing the room.
2. Slowly pan the phone left to right, capturing the entire room.
3. Tilt the phone up and down to capture ceiling and floor.
4. Walk slowly around the room while scanning.
5. Avoid sudden movements — keep the scan smooth.
6. The app will indicate when the scan is complete.

## Step 44: Configure Each MCB — Find Phase Using Multimeter

Click on the **1st MCB**. Options will pop up to configure:

- **Pole Type:** Single Pole (SP), Double Pole (DP), Three Pole (TP), Three Phase + Neutral (TPN)
- **Phase:** Red (A), Yellow (B), Blue (C)
- **Rating:** 6A, 10A, 16A, 20A, 32A, 64A, etc.

Select as per the actual MCB configuration. Physically check the MCB rating printed on it.

### How to Find MCB Phase Using Multimeter

1. **Set Multimeter:** Switch the multimeter roller to **600V AC (~)** — look for the "~" or "V~" symbol on the dial.

2. **Connect Probes:**
   - Black probe → **COM port** on the multimeter
   - Red probe → **Voltage (V) port** on the multimeter

3. **Identify Known Phases:** On the TPN (Three Phase + Neutral) distribution, identify the Red (A), Yellow (B), and Blue (C) phases. These are your reference points.

4. **Test MCB Phase:**
   - Touch **one probe end** on the MCB's live connection (the wire going into the MCB)
   - Touch **other probe end** on a known phase from TPN (e.g., Red/A phase)

5. **Read the Multimeter:**
   - If reading shows **~420V** → That is NOT the same phase (voltage difference between different phases)
   - Keep the probe on the MCB, change the other probe to Phase B (Yellow)
   - If reading shows **~0V** → The MCB is on **Phase B (Yellow)**

6. **Summary:**
   - **~0V reading** = Same phase (MCB is on this phase)
   - **~420V reading** = Different phase (MCB is NOT on this phase)
   - Repeat for all MCBs to determine each one's phase

![MCB Configuration](images/44.png)
*44.png — Configure each MCB with correct phase and rating*

> **Safety Warning:** Be extremely careful when using the multimeter near live electrical connections. Do not touch exposed wires with bare hands. Ensure the multimeter is set to the correct voltage range (600V AC). If unsure, consult a qualified electrician.

# Part I — Post-Audit: Meter Installation Planning

**Goal:** After completing the app audit, assess the physical site for energy meter installation. Check meter placement, CT mounting points, router connectivity, and measure all required distances.

> **Important:** This section must be completed after the app audit is finished. All measurements should be noted in the Google Sheet or notepad for installation planning.

---

## Step 57: Identify Meter Installation Location

Look around the site and identify a suitable location where the **energy meter can be installed**. Consider:

- Proximity to the electrical panel (for CT connections)
- Proximity to router/network hub (for communication)
- Availability of power socket for the meter
- Protection from weather, water, and physical damage
- Accessibility for maintenance and readings

---

## Step 58: Measure Distance — Meter to Panel (CT Mounting Point)

Measure the **approximate distance** between:

- **Meter Installation Place** → **Electrical Panel** (where CTs will be mounted)

Use a measuring tape or pace count to estimate the distance. Note this distance — it determines the CT cable length required.

> **Tip:** If the distance is more than 5 meters, you may need longer CT cables or a different meter placement strategy.

---

## Step 59: Check Router & Network Hub Distance

Locate the **router** and **network hub** on site. Measure the **approximate distance** between:

- **Meter Installation Place** → **Router/Network Hub**

Also check:

- Is there a **power socket** near the meter installation place?
- Is there a **power socket** near the router/network hub?
- Is there an **Ethernet port** or WiFi coverage at the meter location?

Note all these details for installation planning.

---

## Step 60: Inspect Panel for CT Insertion Points

Open the electrical panel (with permission and safety precautions). Look at the panel and identify:

- **Where CTs can be inserted** — around the incoming mains cables
- **Cable size** — to select appropriate CT size
- **Space availability** — is there enough room to clamp CTs around the cables?
- **Cable routing** — how will CT cables run from panel to meter?

---

## Step 61: Measure Distance — Meter to CT Incomer

Measure the **approximate distance** from:

- **Meter Installation Place** → **CT Incomer Point** (where CTs will be clamped on the panel)

This distance is critical for determining the CT cable length and signal integrity.

> **Note:** CT cables should not exceed recommended lengths. If the distance is too long, consider alternative meter placement or consult the technical team.
